Questions, answered.

Is it dishwasher safe?

The body is top-rack safe. Hand-wash the GlowCap — it's sealed, but gentle is better for electronics.

How long does the GlowCap battery last?

About 30 days per charge with default reminders. Charge it with any USB-C cable in about an hour.

Can I turn the reminders off?

Yes. Hold the cap button for three seconds to switch to plain-cap mode. Hold again to turn the glow back on.

What does the lifetime warranty cover?

Insulation failure, leaks and manufacturing defects — for as long as you own it. Dents from adventures are badges of honor.
